# User Stories: "Permanent Lock" Couples App

## Epic 1: Identity Verification & The Permanent Lock
*   **As a new user**, I want to verify my government ID (with a selfie and liveness check) and my phone during onboarding, so that my identity is hashed and tied to a single account. Only hashes are stored, never images or raw numbers. The hashes are wiped only if I complete the 90-day account deletion (Epic 6).
*   **As a user**, I want to send a "Forever Bind" request to my partner using their unique ID handle, so that we can initiate the locking process.
*   **As a partner receiving a bind request**, I want to click through multiple aggressive "Point of No Return" warning screens before accepting, so that I cannot claim I didn't know the connection was irreversible.
*   **As a newly locked couple**, I want to receive a digital "Marriage Certificate" minted to our profiles, so that we have a permanent timestamp of our lock.

## Epic 2: The "Rage-Bait" Restrictions (The Trap)
*   **As a locked user who requests account deletion**, I want my account hidden and the lock to stay active during the 90-day cooldown (see Epic 6), so that deleting the app does not free me early.
*   **As a locked user who wants to link a new partner while still locked**, I want the app to cross-reference my ID hash, instantly block the new connection, and display a "You are already bound" error, so that I cannot use the app with someone else while the lock holds. The lock ends only through Epic 5 (breakup, Divorce Fee) or the day-90 purge.
*   **As an original partner**, I want to receive a push notification and a permanent red flag on our shared dashboard if my partner attempts to verify a new connection, so that their attempt to move on is exposed.
*   **As a new partner trying to link with someone who is already locked**, I want to be shown the profile and photo of the person they are permanently bound to, so that I know exactly who they are locked with.

## Epic 3: The Un-deletable Vault
*   **As a locked user**, I want to upload photos, promises, and voice notes to a shared timeline, so that we can build a digital relationship scrapbook.
*   **As a user who regrets a post**, I want to discover that the "Delete" button is grayed out or non-existent, so that every upload becomes a permanent part of our locked history.
*   **As a locked partner**, I want to see a "Last Seen" and "Location" tag that cannot be toggled off in the privacy settings, so that we are forced into complete, unavoidable transparency.

## Epic 4: The Public Ledger (Social Proof & Drama)
*   **As a single person going on a date**, I want to input my date's phone number or name into the app's "Public Ledger," so that I can check if they are permanently locked to someone else.
*   **As a locked user**, I want my profile to generate a shareable "Locked Status" badge for social media, so that I can publicly prove my extreme commitment.
*   **As a user whose partner tried to delete the app**, I want their profile picture to change to a "Fled the App" placeholder on the Public Ledger during the 90-day cooldown, so that their lack of commitment is publicly shamed.

## Epic 5: Leaving the Lock (Keep the account, ditch the partner)
*   **As a locked user**, I want to click a "Mutual Breakup" button, so that I can initiate a free unlock request to my partner.
*   **As a partner receiving a mutual breakup request**, I want a limited time window (72 hours) to agree, so that we can sever the lock instantly and for free.
*   **As a mutually broken-up user**, I want my shared timeline moved to a private "Past Matches" archive, so that my active profile is instantly cleared and ready for a new "Forever Bind" request.
*   **As a locked user who wants out immediately**, I want the option to pay a $19.99 "Divorce Fee" via in-app purchase, so that I can instantly force a one-sided breakup without my partner's consent.
*   **As the partner of someone who paid the Divorce Fee**, I want to receive an immediate, dramatic push notification that my partner bought their way out of the relationship, so that I know our lock has been severed.

## Epic 6: Account Deletion & The 90-Day Trap (Privacy Compliance)
*   **As any user**, I want to find a free "Delete Account" button in my privacy settings, so that the app complies with international privacy laws (GDPR/CCPA).
*   **As a user requesting account deletion**, I want to be placed into a mandatory 90-day deactivation cooldown, so that my account is hidden but my data is not immediately wiped.
*   **As a locked partner of someone deleting their account**, I want to receive a brutal notification saying "Your partner has initiated account deletion to escape the lock," so that their attempt to flee the app is exposed.
*   **As a user in the 90-day cooldown**, I want the ability to simply log back into the app to cancel the deletion process, so that my account and relationship lock are instantly restored to normal.
*   **As a user who waits out the full 90 days**, I want my ID hash, phone hash, and profile wiped from the database, so that my legal right to be forgotten is honored and I can eventually start over as a brand new user. My partner is freed at that point and keeps their own posts in Past Matches.
