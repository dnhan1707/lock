# User Stories: "Permanent Lock" Couples App

## Epic 1: Identity Verification & The Permanent Lock
*   **As a new user**, I want to scan my government ID and face during onboarding, so that my biometric and legal identity is permanently hashed to a single account for life.
*   **As a user**, I want to send a "Forever Bind" request to my partner using their unique ID handle, so that we can initiate the locking process.
*   **As a partner receiving a bind request**, I want to click through multiple aggressive "Point of No Return" warning screens before accepting, so that I cannot claim I didn't know the connection was irreversible.
*   **As a newly locked couple**, I want to receive a digital "Marriage Certificate" minted to our profiles, so that we have a permanent timestamp of our lock.

## Epic 2: The "Rage-Bait" Restrictions (The Trap)
*   **As a locked user attempting to delete my account**, I want the app to reject my request and simply hide the app from my phone's home screen, so that my locked status remains active on the backend forever.
*   **As a locked user who broke up and wants to link a new partner**, I want the app to cross-reference my ID hash, instantly block the new connection, and display a "You are already bound" error, so that I can never use the app with someone else.
*   **As an original partner**, I want to receive a push notification and a permanent red flag on our shared dashboard if my partner attempts to verify a new connection, so that their attempt to move on is exposed.
*   **As a new partner trying to link with someone who is already locked**, I want to be shown the profile and photo of the person they are permanently bound to, so that I know exactly who they are locked with.

## Epic 3: The Un-deletable Vault
*   **As a locked user**, I want to upload photos, promises, and voice notes to a shared timeline, so that we can build a digital relationship scrapbook.
*   **As a user who regrets a post**, I want to discover that the "Delete" button is grayed out or non-existent, so that every upload becomes a permanent part of our locked history.
*   **As a locked partner**, I want to see a "Last Seen" and "Location" tag that cannot be toggled off in the privacy settings, so that we are forced into complete, unavoidable transparency.

## Epic 4: The Public Ledger (Social Proof & Drama)
*   **As a single person going on a date**, I want to input my date's phone number or name into the app's "Public Ledger," so that I can check if they are permanently locked to someone else.
*   **As a locked user**, I want my profile to generate a shareable "Locked Status" badge for social media, so that I can publicly prove my extreme commitment.
*   **As a user whose partner tried to delete the app**, I want their profile picture to change to a "Fled the App" placeholder on the Public Ledger, so that their lack of commitment is publicly shamed.
